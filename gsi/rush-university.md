**Jay Weinberg, CLU, ChFC** Offers Guardian GSI policies at Rush University · PGY1 Financial Solutions · Updated October 30, 2026

Can Rush University residents get a GSI policy?

Yes. Jay offers a Guardian GSI policy to residents and fellows at Rush University: no health questions, true own-occupation coverage with medical specialty language, and up to $8,000 a month to start, with room to increase later. Get it before you complete any other disability application.

## What Rush University already gives you

Residents here get group disability coverage through their employer. It's a good start, but group policies are usually capped, tied to the job and taxable when they pay out. Jay's view: make an individual policy your primary line of defense and layer the group coverage behind it.

| Long-term disability | Up to $4,000 a month if a disability lasts beyond 90 days, at no cost to you |
| --- | --- |
| Short-term disability | 100% of annual base salary for up to 90 days |
| Extra coverage | Can be purchased at group rates |

Source: [Rush house staff benefits overview 2025-26 (PDF)](https://www.rushu.rush.edu/sites/default/files/2025-07/2025-26_House%20Staff%20Benefits%20Overview_GME_FINAL.pdf). Plan documents govern.

## Jay's Guardian GSI at a glance

| Health questions | None. Guaranteed standard issue, no medical underwriting |
| --- | --- |
| Insurance company | Guardian (issued by Berkshire Life Insurance Company of America, a Guardian subsidiary) |
| Definition of disability | True own occupation to age 67, with medical specialty language |
| Monthly benefit | Up to $8,000, subject to new-professional limits, or up to $15,000 with financial underwriting |
| Increase later | Future Increase Option rider or Benefit Purchase Rider: add coverage later without health questions, up to $15,000 total |
| Waiting period | 90 days |
| Benefits paid to | Age 67 |
| Mental health claims | Paid for up to 24 months |
| Partial disability | Enhanced Partial Benefit rider: triggered by a 15% loss of income, or of time or duties |

From the Guardian GSI brochure on Jay's quote page (2025). Optional riders cost extra, terms vary by state, and offers can change. Jay confirms the current details for Rush University before you apply.

## Get it before any other application

Most GSI policies ask whether you've applied for disability insurance before. If you have, there's a good chance you won't be eligible, so lock in the GSI first, then decide whether to add anything on top.
