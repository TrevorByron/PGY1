    Why check first

    Find out before you fill out any other application

    1. No health questions. The insurance company issues the policy without looking at your medical records, usually within a few days.
    2. Applying elsewhere first can rule you out. Most GSI policies ask whether you've already applied for disability insurance. If you have, there's a good chance you won't be eligible.
    3. Your GME office probably can't tell you. GSI policies are individual policies, not part of your hospital's benefits, so they usually won't show up at open enrollment.
