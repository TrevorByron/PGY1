Can Dartmouth Health residents get a GSI policy?

Yes. Jay offers a Guardian GSI policy to residents and fellows at Dartmouth Health: no health questions, true own-occupation coverage with medical specialty language, and up to $8,000 a month to start, with room to increase later. Get it before you complete any other disability application.

Jay's Guardian GSI at a glance

Health questionsNone. Guaranteed standard issue, no medical underwriting
Insurance companyGuardian (issued by Berkshire Life Insurance Company of America, a Guardian subsidiary)
Definition of disabilityTrue own occupation to age 67, with medical specialty language
Monthly benefitUp to $8,000, subject to new-professional limits, or up to $15,000 with financial underwriting
Increase laterFuture Increase Option rider or Benefit Purchase Rider: add coverage later without health questions, up to $15,000 total
Waiting period90 days
Benefits paid toAge 67
Mental health claimsPaid for up to 24 months
Partial disabilityEnhanced Partial Benefit rider: triggered by a 15% loss of income, or of time or duties

From the Guardian GSI brochure on Jay's quote page (2025). Optional riders cost extra, terms vary by state, and offers can change. Jay confirms the current details for Dartmouth Health before you apply.

Get it before any other application

Most GSI policies ask whether you've applied for disability insurance before. If you have, there's a good chance you won't be eligible, so lock in the GSI first, then decide whether to add anything on top.
