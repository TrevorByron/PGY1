# Talking purposes — marketing and growth

This doc outlines strategy ideas for PGY1 Financial Solutions. None of these are implemented in the codebase; they are for discussion and future implementation.

## SEO

- **Page-level:** Home, About (section on home), Disability 101, Blog (and each post) have or can have unique page titles and meta descriptions.
- **Target terms:** e.g. “physician disability insurance,” “disability insurance residents fellows,” “GSI disability insurance,” “own occupation disability.”
- **Content:** Expand blog and Disability 101 with real articles to support rankings and answers.

## Drip campaign (new leads)

- **Welcome:** Automated email after form submit (e.g. “Thanks — we’ll be in touch within X hours”).
- **Refer a friend:** “Refer a friend and earn $XX” — define amount and ensure compliance with insurance/referral rules.
- **Social proof ask:** “Post a short video of your experience and earn $YY” — define amount and compliance (e.g. disclosure, approval).

## Reviews

- **Google:** Add “Leave a Google review” CTA (link to GMB) after quote or policy completion.
- **Drip:** Optional “How was your experience? Leave a review” email in sequence.

## Social

- **Profiles:** Clean up LinkedIn, etc.; consistent branding and link to site.
- **Content:** Post more; use webinar content — cut webinars into short clips for LinkedIn/Instagram/YouTube with captions and CTAs (e.g. “Get your quote — 1 min, 100% private”).
