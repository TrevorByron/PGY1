Questions/GSI policies

My hospital isn't on the GSI list. Could there still be a GSI policy?

Jay Weinberg, CLU, ChFCPhysician disability insurance specialist · PGY1 Financial SolutionsUpdated October 30, 2026

Short answer

Possibly. If your hospital isn't listed, that does not mean a special disability policy doesn't exist. The list is a short list of a much larger list, and there will be additions in January 2027. Email me at jay@pgy1.com, tell me where you're training, and I can let you know what you may or may not be eligible for.

A short list of a much larger list

Take a quick look and see if your hospital is listed. If it's not, that does not mean by any way, shape or form that a special disability policy does not exist. Once again, this is a short list of a much larger list. And in January 2027, there will be some additions to this list and the greater list as well.

Ask where you're training

If you have any questions about what may or may not be available at your program, drop me a line at jay@pgy1.com. Let me know where you're training and I can let you know what you may or may not be eligible for.

Find out before you apply anywhere

Find out if there's an exclusive GSI policy for the residents and fellows where you're training. Just because there is one doesn't mean any insurance professional can offer it, so you also need to find one that can. And if you apply for a medically underwritten policy before getting a GSI policy, you will likely disqualify yourself from GSI eligibility.
