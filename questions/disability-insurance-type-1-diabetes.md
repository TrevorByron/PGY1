# Can I get disability insurance as a resident with type 1 diabetes?

**Jay Weinberg, CLU, ChFC** Physician disability insurance specialist · PGY1 Financial Solutions · Updated October 30, 2026

Short answer

It depends on who you go to. If you go to an insurance professional that can't offer you a GSI policy, it's highly unlikely you'd be able to get a policy. You'd likely be declined. If you go to a GSI agent and you haven't previously disqualified yourself, you can likely have a very comprehensive policy within one week.

## A non-GSI agent

Unfortunately, I see situations like this all the time. Let's say we have a resident that's a type 1 diabetic. If you go to an insurance professional that cannot offer you a GSI policy, it's highly unlikely that you would be able to get a policy. You would likely be declined.

## A GSI agent

Now, if you go to a GSI agent and you have not previously disqualified yourself, you can likely have yourself a very, very comprehensive policy within one week that has all the right bells, whistles and features. With a medically underwritten policy, they look at your medical records, the pharmacy database, insurance company billing codes and family history. With a GSI policy, they simply ask you a few eligibility questions.

## Don't disqualify yourself first

If you apply for a medically underwritten policy prior to getting a GSI policy, you will likely disqualify yourself from GSI eligibility. So before engaging an insurance professional, ask two questions. Is there a GSI policy available to the residents and fellows at my hospital? And if there is, can you offer it?
