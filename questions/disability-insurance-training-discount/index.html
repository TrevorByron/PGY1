Is disability insurance cheaper if I buy it during training?

Jay Weinberg, CLU, ChFCPhysician disability insurance specialist · PGY1 Financial SolutionsUpdated October 30, 2026

Short answer

Often, yes. Many insurance companies have discounts on these policies if you get one while you're in your training years. You lock in the discount, and it goes with the policy for the duration.

Two reasons to get a policy in training

Secure a policy when you're in training. You know what your health is now. You can't tell me what your health is going to be when you're done training. You get a really small policy now, and you have the ability to balloon that policy up on the back end. That's reason number one.

Reason number two: many insurance companies have discounts on these policies if you get one while you're in your training years. So you get one now, lock in the discount, and that discount is going to go with the policy for the duration.

You're not going to go broke

Maybe it's $60, $80, $100 a month for these policies. You're not going to end up in the poorhouse.
