# Is disability insurance more expensive in California?

**Jay Weinberg, CLU, ChFC** Physician disability insurance specialist · PGY1 Financial Solutions · Updated October 30, 2026

Short answer

Usually, yes. Insurance pricing and wording are regulated state by state, and California is the state that stands out for higher pricing, modified policies, or both. If you're heading to California, consider getting a policy before you move.

## Why the state matters

Insurance pricing and wording are set state by state. I'm a broker and work with different companies, each with its own nuances, but California is consistent: most companies charge a surcharge there, modify the policy, or both.

## Three situations

**Moving to California.** Strongly consider getting the process going before you move.

**In California now, matched somewhere else.** I'm never going to tell you to wait. Compare what the policy looks like if you get it now with what it would look like in your new state in a couple of months, then decide.

**In California now and staying.** You'll likely just have to grin and bear it.
